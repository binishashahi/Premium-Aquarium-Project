Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I choose the right aquarium size?

Consider the number and size of fish, space available, and maintenance capability.

2. How often should I clean the aquarium?

Partial water changes should be done weekly, and full cleaning monthly depending on tank size.

3. What fish are best for beginners?

Betta fish, guppies, and goldfish are popular beginner-friendly fish.
